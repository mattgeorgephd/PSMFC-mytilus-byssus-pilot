# 03_analyses

| Subfolder | Produced by | Contents |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| `hisat/` | `01_hisat_stringtie.Rmd` (HPC) | the reference StringTie tables (`t_data.ctab`, `e_data.ctab`, `i_data.ctab`, `e2t.ctab`, `i2t.ctab`) and MultiQC alignment-rate reports. The per-sample StringTie folders (GTF and ctabs) and BAMs are not committed (too large); `t_data.ctab` gives transcript lengths to `07_enrichment` and the transcript-to-gene map to `02_prepDE.Rmd` |
| `isoform-gene-map/` | `04_isoform_gene_map.Rmd` | each Iso-Seq isoform's genome gene (or novel locus, mitochondrial feature, or none), with a summary and the agreement with the retired CDS-based map; the minimap2 alignments are git-ignored (README inside) |
| `augmented-annotation/` | `05_augmented_annotation.Rmd` | option B: the RefSeq annotation augmented with the isoforms (`ext3`, 3' extension; `full`, full models), with the rules' results; the GFF and SAF files are git-ignored (README inside) |
| `genome-recount/` | `06_genome_recount.Rmd` | option B: the reads realigned and counted on each annotation by two counters (six gene count matrices; featureCounts on `ext3` is the source of the record), mapping summary, checks; the HISAT2 index and per-library files are git-ignored (README inside) |
| `featurecounts/` | `07_count_matrix_of_record.Rmd` | `gene_count_matrix.csv`, the count matrix of record and the input of `05_differential-expression`, and `RUN_provenance.txt` (README inside) |
| `mitogenome/` | `08_mitogenome_counts.Rmd` | the mitochondrial genes counted on the mitochondrial genome alone (`mitogenome_gene_counts.csv`, of record, and `mitogenome_gene_counts_permissive.csv`), `mapping_summary.csv`, `RUN_provenance.txt`; `libraries/` is git-ignored (README inside) |
| `prepDE/` | `prepDE.py` (HPC) and `02_prepDE.Rmd` | `transcript_count_matrix.csv` and `gene_count_matrix.csv` of the HPC alignment: the previous record (until 2026-10-02), now read by `06_iso-seq-transcriptome` step 04 for comparison (README inside) |
| `fastqc/trimmed/`, `fastqc/untrimmed/` | FastQC 0.12.1 and MultiQC 1.12 (`byssus-exp-analysis/code/01-diff-exp-analysis.Rmd`, December 2023) | per-library read quality reports (HTML + ZIP) and the MultiQC summary (`multiqc_report.html`, `multiqc_data/`). `trimmed/`: the reads analysed (`*_L099_R1_cmb.trim.fastq.gz`), 131 libraries. `untrimmed/`: the raw reads on gannet, 145 lane files of 73 libraries (T030G has lane 1 only) |
| `read_trimming/` | `03_read_trimming.Rmd` | `read_retention.csv`: per library, raw reads (both lanes; blank where gannet has none), trimmed reads, their mean length and the share retained. `recipe_check.csv`: three candidate trimming recipes run on the first 55,576 raw reads of T001F lane 1, each compared read by read with the trimmed file (the Matz-lab clipper plus `fastx_clipper` reproduces 99.8% exactly). `RUN_provenance_recipe_check.txt`: sample URLs, tool versions, clipper MD5 |
| `fastqc/multiqc_report_trimmed_merged_2022-08-09.html` | MultiQC 1.12 (FastQC module), August 2022; copied from gannet `panopea/PSMFC-mytilus-byssus-pilot/` | QC of a different, earlier trimming of the first 73 libraries: cutadapt with a fixed 15-base 5' cut and no deduplication, lanes concatenated (`../01_code/_superseded/1_1_process-tagseq-data-mytilus.Rmd`); 2.5 to 6.3 million reads, mean length 71 to 78 bp. These are not the reads analysed: those (`*_cmb.trim`, all 131 libraries) are in `fastqc/trimmed/` and were made as `../README.md` ("The reads") describes |
| `_superseded/kallisto/` | `_superseded/07-kallisto.Rmd` | kallisto quant per sample; superseded by HISAT2 |
| `_superseded/02_isoform-gene-map_cds/` | `../01_code/_superseded/02_isoform_gene_map_cds.Rmd` | the retired CDS-based isoform-to-gene map (README inside; made in `06_iso-seq-transcriptome` and moved here with the current map on 2026-10-03); the alignments are git-ignored; superseded by `isoform-gene-map/`, whose `cds_map_agreement.csv` compares the two maps |
| `knit_html/` | the runner | HTML reports and logs (git-ignored) |
